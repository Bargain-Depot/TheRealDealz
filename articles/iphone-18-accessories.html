Before you buy

Check the exact model name.

“iPhone 18” is too vague for a case. Confirm Pro, Pro Max, or Duo before checkout.

MagSafe is not the whole story on Duo.

A hinge cover or folding layout can change how wallets, stands, and mounts physically sit even when magnetic charging is supported.

Do not buy every category.

A case, screen protection, and the charger setup you actually use are enough for most people. Everything else should solve a specific habit.
