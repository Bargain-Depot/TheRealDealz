Ray-Ban Meta Audio vs Gen 3: check the trade-offs before you preorder

Verified September 24, 2026. Meta's Connect launch created a useful buyer decision: camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio starts at $349 and ships October 13, while Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 3) starts at $449 and is available now.

The $100 question

Meta Audio is the privacy- and audio-first option. Meta says it has no camera, weighs 43 grams, offers up to 12 hours of use on one charge plus up to 48 additional hours from the included charging case, and comes in Clubmaster and Burbank styles across 23 color/lens combinations. Both styles support a broad range of prescription lenses.

Gen 3 costs $100 more at starting price and keeps the camera. Meta says it offers up to nine hours of battery life, a 12 MP camera with 3K Ultra HD video, a new six-microphone array, and Aviator, Zena and Wayfarer frame options.

Which one looks like the better value?

Choose Meta Audio if your priority is open-ear listening, calls and hands-free AI without wearing a camera. Its longer claimed battery life and lower starting price make it the cleaner value proposition for that use case.

Choose Gen 3 if hands-free photo/video capture is a major reason you want smart glasses. Paying the extra $100 primarily makes sense when the camera and expanded capture features matter to you.
