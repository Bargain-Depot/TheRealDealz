The shortcut

Buy the $129 model if USB-C charging is already normal for you and you mostly control volume from your phone or watch. Spend the extra $20 if you use wireless chargers, adjust volume from your earbuds, or regularly misplace the case. Those are the upgrades you will actually notice day to day.
