What every Googlebook includes

Google says every Googlebook includes 12 months of Google AI Pro with 5TB of cloud storage, plus three months of YouTube Premium and Adobe Photoshop. Googlebook OS is slated for feature drops and updates for up to 10 years. These are launch entitlements and terms can apply.
