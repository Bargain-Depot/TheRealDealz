Avoid the markup trap

Third-party bundles and marketplace offers can be dramatically above retail. Compare against the $899 verified retail reference before buying. Do not assume a higher price means a scarce or upgraded official configuration.

Do you actually need Pro for GTA VI?

No. PlayStation's official GTA VI page says the game releases on both PS5 and PS5 Pro. It is listed as PS5 Pro Enhanced, but a regular PS5 is still a supported platform. That makes the standard PS5 a legitimate alternative if Pro stock disappears or resale pricing spikes.
